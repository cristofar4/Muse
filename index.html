Start with what you already love: a season, a place, a flower. Then try it on skin rather than paper, because a fragrance changes with your warmth over the first hour. The scent finder above turns three questions into a match.

Yes. The discovery set holds all three signatures in small vials, enough to wear each one for several days and see how it settles on you before choosing a full bottle.

Upright, capped, and away from sunlight and heat. A drawer or a cool shelf is kinder than a bathroom windowsill. Stored this way, an eau de parfum keeps its character for years.

Expect six to eight hours from an eau de parfum. Spray onto pulse points and let it dry without rubbing, which breaks up the top notes before they can open.
